It collects 95% of the buyers' pot while the winner receives nothing. Combined, this is worse than either alone: M-1 alone needs organic demand >= reserve, and the flip alone still leaves buyers a chance at the NFT. It is not reported as a separate finding because one untrusted actor (the sponsor) controls both legs, and the only trust boundary crossed is M-1's prize-contract boundary.
