## 1. Executive Summary of Reconciliation

After sealing `FIRST-PASS.md` and `FIRST-PASS.md.sha256` at `2026-10-21T08:46:56Z` using strictly clean-context source-only inputs, the Gemini lane unsealed the repository's historical audit artifacts (`packages/contracts/audit/**`) and test suites (`packages/contracts/test/**`), executed dynamic verification (`forge test`, `slither`, `echidna`, `python3 unittest`, and custom PoCs in `GeminiAuditPoC.t.sol`), and ran `GEMINI-GEBAL/attempt-02` (test-inclusive mode) with `gebal compare`.

### Key Takeaways

1. **Both Medium First-Pass Findings (`FP-M-01` and `FP-M-02`) are 100% True Positives and Dynamically Verified by Foundry PoCs**:
   - Both findings touch architectural seams that the protocol team previously wrestled with across `FINDINGS.md`, `ETHSKILLS-REVIEW-2026-08-13.md`, `V12-REVIEW-2026-08-20.md`, and `CURRENT-RESIDUAL-RISKS.md`, where prior mitigations were either rolled back (`ES-01` removed `recoverProtocolOwnedClaim`) or downgraded to off-chain operational assumptions (`V12-243754` removed the `ES-03` NFT delivery gate), leaving exploitable or specification-violating behavior in the on-chain contracts.
   - Even more importantly, `FP-M-01` identified **two specific vectors not tested in `Raffle.t.sol`**: while the existing test `testFutureCanonicalCloneCanBrickOnlyItsOwnWinnerRedemption` only tested a *buyer* passing a future clone address to `buyEntries`, our cold first-pass audit discovered that setting `sponsorRecipient` to a future clone's `CREATE` address both **permanently bricks `releaseSponsorProceeds()`** (`InvalidQuoteDestination`) AND **transfers the prize NFT directly into the second Raffle clone via `releaseSponsorPrize()`** (or `redeemWinningTicket()` in `Status.NftWon`), directly violating `DESIGN.md`'s explicit invariant *"Never move assets between raffle clones"*.
2. **All Three Low First-Pass Findings (`FP-L-01`, `FP-L-02`, `FP-L-03`) are Novel True Positives Not Documented in `packages/contracts/audit/**`**:
   - **`FP-L-01`**: Discontinuous payout cliff at `totalEntries == reserveEntries` (sponsor share jumps from 15% in `CashWon` to 95% in `NftWon`) combined with unrestricted sponsor self-purchase (`buyEntries`) and single-buyer (`ticketCount == 1`) under-target `requestDraw` fee extraction (20% fee on a 1-buyer failed raffle). Verified dynamically in `testPoC_FPL01_SponsorSelfPurchaseAndSingleBuyerExtraction`.
   - **`FP-L-02`**: Front-running sponsor pre-`endTime` empty-raffle cancellation (`enableRefunds`) with a 1-USDC `buyEntries` call, which forces `enableRefunds()` to revert with `RefundsNotAvailable(endTime + DRAW_REQUEST_TIMEOUT, block.timestamp)` and locks the sponsor's NFT for up to 32 days (or wins it for 1 USDC if `reserveEntries == 1`). Verified dynamically in `testPoC_FPL02_FrontrunEmptyRaffleCancelWith1USDC`.
   - **`FP-L-03`**: Mid-escrow callback reentrancy during `RaffleFactory.createRaffle` where `Raffle.onERC721Received` transitions `status` to `Status.Active` before `prize.safeTransferFrom` returns and while `Raffle`'s own `ReentrancyGuard` is unentered, allowing `prizeToken` to call `raffle.buyEntries` before `RaffleCreated` is emitted and before post-transfer `prize.ownerOf` verification. Verified dynamically in `testPoC_FPL03_MidEscrowBuyEntriesBeforeRaffleCreated`.
3. **Informational Finding (`FP-I-01`) Uncovered an Undocumented Regression from `ES-02`**:
   - In `ETHSKILLS-REVIEW-2026-08-13.md` (`ES-02`), the protocol team noted that secondary ticket transfers were locked once `Drawing` started, leaving only the `requestDraw` transition boundary exposed. However, in the Ethereum v1 rewrite (`Raffle.sol:497-502`), `Raffle._update` no longer blocks transfers during `Status.Drawing` or `Status.NftWon`/`Status.CashWon`, reopening the exact mempool front-running window during `Status.Drawing` when the Chainlink VRF fulfillment transaction becomes visible!

## 3. Detailed Reconciliation of Historical Audit Findings (`packages/contracts/audit/**`)

To ensure complete coverage, we also reconciled every historical finding in `packages/contracts/audit/FINDINGS.md`, `ETHSKILLS-REVIEW-2026-08-13.md`, and `V12-REVIEW-2026-08-20.md` against the frozen commit `9238b6ce8eb7f768c0dcd0cfcef26e1427a03155`:

1. **`H-01` (Missing `ReentrancyGuard` initialization in clones)**: Mitigated in current code via standard OpenZeppelin `ReentrancyGuard` (though as noted in `FP-I-01`, `_status` starts at `0` in clones rather than `1`, costing an extra 15,000 gas on the first `buyEntries` call compared to `ReentrancyGuardTransient`).
2. **`H-02` (Prize deposit uses `transferFrom` instead of `safeTransferFrom` + `onERC721Received` + `ownerOf` verification)**: Partially mitigated in `RaffleFactory.sol:140-145`, with residual mid-escrow activation/event ordering defect captured in our novel finding **`FP-L-03`**.
3. **`H-03` / `H-04` / `M-01` / `ES-01` / `V12-243748..243750, 243759` (Protocol/clone destinations locking claims or moving assets between clones)**: Partially mitigated for *already-deployed* contracts via `_isProtocolDestination` (`destination.code.length != 0`), but **unmitigated for precomputed future `CREATE` clone addresses** and omitted in `releaseSponsorPrize()` and `redeemWinningTicket()`, as proven in **`FP-M-01`**.
4. **`M-02` / `V12-243756` (Dynamic VRF native fee vs. forced ETH / selfdestruct balance)**: Properly mitigated in `Raffle.sol:252-270` (`requestDraw` checks `msg.value < requestPrice` directly, snapshots `balanceBefore = address(this).balance - msg.value`, and refunds `address(this).balance - balanceBefore` via `_refundNative`). Verified by our cold audit across all lanes (`GEMINI-SIMAO`, `GEMINI-PASHOV`, `GEMINI-TOB`) as safe.
5. **`M-03` (ERC-20 decimals check in `RaffleFactory` constructor)**: Properly mitigated in `RaffleFactory.sol:100-104` (`_readTokenDecimals` staticcall checking `decimals == 6`).
6. **`M-04` / `V12-243751` (Sequential ERC-721 ticket receipts vs. per-entry minting)**: Properly mitigated in `Raffle.sol:224-233` (`buyEntries` mints one sequential `ticketId` per purchase with `TicketRange(startEntry, endEntry)`).
7. **`ES-02` (Ticket transfer front-running around draw / VRF callback)**: **Regressed in Ethereum v1 (`Raffle._update`)** — while `ES-02` stated that transfers were locked during `Drawing`, `Raffle._update` now allows transfers during both `Drawing` and `NftWon`/`CashWon`. Captured in **`FP-I-01`**.
8. **`ES-03` / `V12-243754` (Non-transferable/paused prize NFT after settlement)**: **Regressed in Ethereum v1 (`Raffle.releaseSponsorProceeds`)** — `ES-03` gated sponsor proceeds on verified NFT delivery with a 30-day refund fallback, whereas Ethereum v1 removed the gate and allows the sponsor to claim 95% of buyer USDC even when `redeemWinningTicket` permanently reverts. Captured in **`FP-M-02`**.
9. **`V12-243752` / `V12-243753` / `V12-243757` (Chainlink VRF v2.5 callback authentication, requestId binding, word count, and strict `< callbackDeadline` inequality)**: Properly mitigated in `Raffle.sol:457-494` (`fulfillRandomWords` called only via `VRFV2PlusWrapperConsumerBase.rawFulfillRandomWords`, checks `status == Status.Drawing`, `requestId == pendingRequestId`, `randomWords.length == 1`, `block.timestamp < callbackDeadline`).
