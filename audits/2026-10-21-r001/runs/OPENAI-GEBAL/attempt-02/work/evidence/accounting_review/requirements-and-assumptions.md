# Requirements and assumptions stated before disposition

The authoritative requirements are the clean design specification in the native context export. They require immutable factory and clone targets; one transferable ERC721 range ticket per purchase; exactly 1,000,000 raw canonical USDC units per entry; atomic escrow of exactly the configured ERC721 prize; reserve-met NFT plus 95% sponsor / 5% treasury; reserve-missed cash allocation of 80% winner / 15% sponsor / 5% treasury; complete refunds and prize return on refund outcomes; aggregate floor fee rounding with the remainder allocated; no administration or upgrade; strict request and callback deadlines; callback-only result recording; permissionless storage-only settlement; current-owner redemption with atomic claim burn and rollback on delivery failure; independent sponsor and treasury claims; and no asset movement between raffle clones.

The explicit trust boundary is canonical quote token and Chainlink wrapper trusted; arbitrary prize contracts and ticket recipients untrusted. I assume standard non-rebasing, fee-free transfer behavior of the trusted canonical token. Deployment identity of those dependencies is not established by a six-decimal metadata or code-length check. The assessed contract code, rather than NatSpec or prose, determines actual behavior. A malicious arbitrary prize contract may lie about its own state; local ownerOf checks can confirm only its reported state and cannot establish that contract's honest implementation. I do not assume untrusted recipients cooperate with callbacks or claims.

Accounting properties considered: purchase liability equals entry count times price; ticket ranges form a gap-free disjoint cover of sold entries; settlement/refund branches conserve accounted quote liabilities; duplicate redemption and duplicate refund cannot succeed; direct donations remain outside sales; no external failure can irreversibly consume an otherwise valid claim; and one recipient's failure must not prevent another independent claim.

Authorization properties considered: mint payment may originate from the purchaser while the recipient owns the claim; approved ERC721 operators may transfer tickets but redemption requires the current owner; initialization is one-time and factory-only; authentic VRF fulfillment chooses a sold entry only once; permissionless settlement cannot direct funds away from fixed/current-owner recipients.

Lifecycle and bounded-exit properties considered: sales stop at endTime; request window is [endTime, endTime+2 days); callback window ends strictly at request timestamp+2 days; deadline equality enables refunds; every terminal branch has bounded per-claim work; and non-cooperative prize/recipient callbacks cannot corrupt other liabilities.

This is a cold source-only specialist pass. Before forming conclusions I have not read root or peer review outputs, historical findings, scanner outputs, test output, deployment data, or source outside the native context. No assessed code, generated tests, exploits, or transactions will be executed. Requested model selection is gpt-6-astra with model_reasoning_effort ultra; provider-returned model identity, effective runtime effort, token usage, and cost are unknown to this reviewer.
