## REQ-11 — No administrator, mutable configuration, or upgrade

> "Ethereum NFT raffle; immutable factory, ERC-1167 clones with immutable implementation."
> "No administrator, mutable configuration or upgrade."
> — DESIGN.md # Clean design specification, L2

> "The implementation, canonical six-decimal USDC, treasury, fee, and Chainlink configuration are immutable. The factory has no owner, pause, upgrade, rescue, or mutable protocol configuration."
> — packages/contracts/src/RaffleFactory.sol, L18-L19 (and packages/contracts/src/interfaces/IRaffleFactory.sol, L7)

> "Each raffle is a fixed, non-upgradeable ERC-1167 clone with no administrator or rescue path."
> — packages/contracts/src/Raffle.sol, L24-L25

**Verdict:** implemented · confidence: high

**What this demands of an implementation:**
1. **No administrator or owner role:** Neither `RaffleFactory` nor `Raffle` (nor any base contract they inherit from) may define an owner, administrator, governance, or privileged operator role.
2. **No mutable configuration:** All factory-level configuration (`quoteToken`, `vrfWrapper`, `protocolTreasury`, `raffleImplementation`, fee rates, and timing/gas constants) must be `constant` or `immutable`. All clone-level raffle configuration (`sponsor`, `sponsorRecipient`, `protocolTreasury`, `prizeToken`, `prizeTokenId`, `raffleId`, `reserveEntries`, `endTime`) must be written at most once during clone initialization and never modified thereafter; the implementation contract itself must be locked against initialization.
3. **No pause or rescue privilege:** Neither contract may include pause gates or functions capable of sweeping/rescuing ERC-20 tokens, ERC-721 tokens, or native currency outside the deterministic raffle lifecycle claims.
4. **No upgrade mechanism:** Neither `RaffleFactory` nor `Raffle` clones may change their implementation address or execute arbitrary `delegatecall` / `selfdestruct` upgrades.

**How the verdict was reached:**
The requirement is `implemented` (and not `partial` or `contradicted`) because an exhaustive review of the inheritance hierarchy, storage layout, state-changing functions, and `msg.sender` checks in both `RaffleFactory` and `Raffle` confirms that:
1. Neither contract defines or inherits an owner or administrator role.
2. All factory parameters are `constant` or `immutable`, the shared `Raffle` implementation is locked in its constructor (`initialized = true`), and all per-raffle configuration fields in `Raffle` are written exclusively in the one-time `onlyFactory` `initialize` call during `createRaffle`.
3. Neither contract contains any pause mechanism or any function to rescue/sweep arbitrary ERC-20, ERC-721, or native tokens.
4. `RaffleFactory` is non-upgradeable with an `immutable raffleImplementation`, and each raffle is deployed via `Clones.clone` as a fixed-target EIP-1167 minimal proxy with no upgrade path.
