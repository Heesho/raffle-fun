## REQ-15-4 — `onERC721Received` requires ERC-721 transfer `operator` to be the canonical `factory`

> "ERC-721 transfer operator, which must be the canonical factory."

**Verdict:** implemented · confidence: high

**What this demands of an implementation:**

When `Raffle.onERC721Received(address operator, address from, uint256 tokenId, bytes calldata)` is invoked, the ERC-721 transfer `operator` parameter must equal the raffle's immutable canonical `factory` address (`Raffle.sol:L55`, `Raffle.sol:L161`).
