Disclaimer

This whitepaper explains how the raffle.fun smart contracts work at one reviewed source-code commit. It is an explanatory document, not an offer, a promise of future performance, or an inducement to participate. The protocol distributes a prize by chance; gambling, sweepstakes, lottery, consumer-protection, sanctions, tax, and licensing laws treat chance-based prize systems differently across jurisdictions, and nothing here is legal, tax, investment, or financial advice. Anyone who operates or uses the protocol must obtain advice for their own jurisdiction. Smart-contract testing and internal review reduce risk but cannot eliminate it, and this document describes unaudited software: do not commit assets you cannot afford to lose unless and until the protocol has received appropriate independent review and production validation. Raffle tickets are entries in a prize drawing. They are not investments, they carry no expectation of profit, and most tickets win nothing.
