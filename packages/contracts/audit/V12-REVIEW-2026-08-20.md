# V12 audit-export review — 2026-08-20

## Decision

**No confirmed in-scope vulnerability was identified in the supplied V12 export. This
is not a mainnet go-ahead and does not replace the required independent audit of a
frozen release SHA.**

Every exported entry is marked `Severity: Low` and `Validity: Invalid`. Independent
review against the recorded source found no basis to promote any of the fifteen entries
to a confirmed in-scope exploit. Some entries accurately describe accepted trust
assumptions, user-controlled self-stranding, or deployment misconfiguration risks and
remain useful as residual-risk evidence.

No source change was made as part of this review.

## Important retained risks

The invalid finding labels must not be read as guarantees that arbitrary dependencies
or recipients are safe:

- A malicious or later-hostile ERC-721 can lie about custody or permanently block prize
  delivery. The protocol cannot independently prove a collection's economic honesty.
- Official USDC and Chainlink addresses, runtime, configuration, and availability remain
  release and operational trust roots.
- A sponsor or ticket owner can strand its own fixed recipient or bearer credential at
  an incapable or future address.
- A bad immutable treasury configuration can poison a factory; the deployment validator
  and human treasury review are mandatory controls.

These items are already within the limitations described by `SECURITY.md`,
`docs/THREAT-MODEL.md`, and `CURRENT-RESIDUAL-RISKS.md`.

## Follow-up assurance work

These are hardening and evidence improvements, not confirmed fixes required by the V12
export:

1. Add direct refund regressions for a duplicate ID in one batch, a nonexistent ID, and
   a successful exactly-100-ticket batch.
2. Add a fully lying ERC-721 mock to demonstrate the documented prize-token trust
   boundary end to end.
3. Either require a code-bearing treasury in the factory constructor or retain the
   deployment-only policy and add a direct predicted-treasury/repeated-creation
   regression for `243750`.
4. Add prominent per-raffle prize-collection verification or warning at the purchase
   decision, not only in general documentation.
5. Document `ticketRange()` explicitly as persistent historical range metadata and use
   `ownerOf()` when live-ticket semantics are required.
